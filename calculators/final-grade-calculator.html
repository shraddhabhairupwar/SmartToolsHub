Final Grade Calculator

Select how you want to calculate your grade.

Grade Scale

90 - 100 = A+ (Outstanding)

80 - 89.99 = A (Excellent)

70 - 79.99 = B+ (Very Good)

60 - 69.99 = B (Good)

50 - 59.99 = C (Average)

40 - 49.99 = D (Pass)

Below 40 = F (Fail)


Example

Marks Method

Total Marks = 500
Obtained Marks = 425
Percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

Result → Grade A (Excellent)
