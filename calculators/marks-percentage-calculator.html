Marks Percentage Calculator

Calculate your percentage using obtained marks and total marks.

Formula

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Enter total marks and obtained marks to calculate percentage.


Example

Total Marks = 500

Obtained Marks = 425

Percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100

Percentage = 85%
