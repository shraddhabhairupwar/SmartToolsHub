Attendance Percentage Calculator

Calculate your attendance percentage instantly.

Formula

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Enter total classes and attended classes to calculate attendance percentage.


Example

Total Classes = 120

Classes Attended = 96

Attendance Percentage = (96 ÷ 120) × 100

Attendance Percentage = 80%
