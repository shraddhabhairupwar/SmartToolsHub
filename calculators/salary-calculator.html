Salary Calculator (CTC to In-Hand)

Calculate your monthly and annual in-hand salary instantly.

Formula

Monthly Gross Salary = Annual CTC ÷ 12

Monthly PF = (Annual CTC × PF%) ÷ 100 ÷ 12

Monthly Income Tax = Annual Income Tax ÷ 12

Monthly In-Hand Salary = Monthly Gross Salary − Monthly PF − Professional Tax − Monthly Income Tax


Example

Annual CTC = ₹600000

PF = 12%

Professional Tax = ₹200 / Month

Income Tax = ₹0

Monthly In-Hand Salary ≈ ₹43,800
