Loan Eligibility Calculator

Estimate the maximum loan amount you may be eligible for.

Formula

Eligible EMI = (Monthly Income × 50%) − Existing EMI

Loan Eligibility is calculated based on the eligible EMI, annual interest rate and loan tenure.


Example

Monthly Income = ₹50,000

Existing EMI = ₹5,000

Interest Rate = 8.5%

Loan Tenure = 20 Years

Estimated Loan Eligibility ≈ ₹23,50,000
