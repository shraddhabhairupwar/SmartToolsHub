Calorie Calculator

Calculate your estimated daily calorie requirement instantly.

Formula

Male BMR = (10 × Weight) + (6.25 × Height) − (5 × Age) + 5

Female BMR = (10 × Weight) + (6.25 × Height) − (5 × Age) − 161

Daily Calories = BMR × Activity Factor


Example

Gender = Female

Age = 25 Years

Weight = 60 kg

Height = 165 cm

Activity = Moderately Active

Daily Calories ≈ 2,085 kcal
