DBATU CGPA Calculator

Calculate your DBATU CGPA using semester SGPA and credits.

Formula

CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Σ(Semester Credits)

Enter each semester SGPA and total credits to calculate CGPA.


Example

Semester 1 SGPA = 8.2

Semester 2 SGPA = 8.5

Semester 3 SGPA = 8.7

Final CGPA is calculated based on semester credits.
