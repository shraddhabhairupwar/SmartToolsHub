CGPA Calculator

Calculate your CGPA using grade points and credits.

Formula

CGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Enter the grade point and credits for each subject.


Example

Subject 1 = Grade Point 8.5, Credits 4

Subject 2 = Grade Point 9.0, Credits 3

CGPA = (8.5 × 4 + 9 × 3) ÷ (4 + 3)

CGPA = 61 ÷ 7

CGPA = 8.71
