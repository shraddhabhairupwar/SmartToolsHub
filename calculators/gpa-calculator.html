GPA Calculator

Calculate your GPA using subject grades and credit points.

Formula

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Point) ÷ Σ(Credit Point)

Enter each subject grade point and credit to calculate GPA.


Example

Subject 1: Grade Point = 9, Credit = 4

Subject 2: Grade Point = 8, Credit = 3

Subject 3: Grade Point = 10, Credit = 3

GPA = (9×4 + 8×3 + 10×3) ÷ (4+3+3)

GPA = 9.00
