Compound Interest Calculator

Calculate compound interest and total amount instantly.

Formula

A = P × (1 + R / (100 × n))n × t

Compound Interest = A − P


Example

Principal = ₹10,000

Rate = 8%

Time = 5 Years

Frequency = Yearly

Compound Interest = ₹4,693.28

Total Amount = ₹14,693.28
