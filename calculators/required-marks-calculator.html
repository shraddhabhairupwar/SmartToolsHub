Required Marks Calculator

Calculate the marks you need to achieve your target percentage.

Formula

Target Marks = (Total Marks × Target Percentage) ÷ 100

Required Marks = Target Marks − Marks Already Obtained

Remaining Exam Marks = Total Marks − Completed Marks


Example

Total Marks = 500

Target Percentage = 75%

Marks Obtained = 280

Completed Marks = 400

Required Marks = 95 out of remaining 100 marks
