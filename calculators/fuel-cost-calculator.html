Fuel Cost / Trip Cost Calculator

Calculate fuel required and total trip cost instantly.

Formula

Fuel Required = Distance ÷ Mileage

Trip Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price


Example

Distance = 250 km

Mileage = 20 km/L

Fuel Price = ₹105/L

Fuel Required = 12.5 L

Trip Cost = ₹1,312.50
